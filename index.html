Wallrace

Reach the far side first. Block with walls, but never seal anyone in.

Blue 10
Red 10

Click a highlighted square to move. Point at a groove between squares to place a wall. At a crossing, press R or right-click to rotate. On touch screens, tap once to preview a wall and again to place it.

    Rules
    • 9×9 board. Blue starts at the bottom, Red at the top. Blue moves first.
    • Each turn, either move your pawn one square or place one wall. You have 10 walls.
    • A wall is two squares long. Walls can't overlap or cross.
    • A wall may never cut off a player's last route to their goal row.
    • If the pawns are face to face, you can jump over the other pawn. If a wall or the edge is behind it, step diagonally instead.
    • The first to reach the opposite row wins.
